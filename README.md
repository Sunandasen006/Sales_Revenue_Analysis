Sales Report Analysis (FY 2025)

===============================

This repository holds my Excel data analysis project, 'Sales_Report_Analysis.xlsx'.

I put it together to explore 5,000 sales orders from 2025 and track how revenue,

costs, and profit margins perform across different regions and product categories.


Quick Highlights from the Data

------------------------------

- Total Orders   : 5,000 (32,314 units sold)

- Total Revenue  : INR 287.40 Million

- Total Profit   : INR 85.59 Million (29.78% overall margin)

- Top Region     : West brought in the highest revenue, while North had the best profit margin (30.35%).

- Top Categories: Electronics drove over 60% of total sales, while Office Supplies and Accessories had the highest profit margins (~47-48%).



What's Inside the Workbook (5 Sheets)

-------------------------------------

1. Dashboard         - A clean summary view of the main KPIs.

2. Sales_Data        - The raw dataset with all 5,000 orders and 18 columns.

3. Pivot_Analysis    - Sales and profit breakdowns by region and category.

4. Formula_Solutions - The Excel formulas used to calculate the metrics.

5. Project_Overview  - Notes on the dataset structure and how to use the file.



How to View It

--------------

Just download 'Sales_Report_Analysis.xlsx' and open it in Microsoft Excel.

The summary tables are formula driven, so any filters or updates you make in 'Sales_Data' will refresh automatically.
